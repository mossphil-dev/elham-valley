Kent Business Partnership Supports Elham Valley Railway Heritage

The Elham Valley Line Trust is delighted to announce a new fundraising partnership with The Vinyl Biz, an independent family-run vinyl graphics business based locally in Lyminge, Kent.

Through this partnership, The Vinyl Biz will donate 10% of the value of qualifying orders placed using the code EVLT10 to the Elham Valley Line Trust.

The money raised will help support our ongoing work preserving the history of the Elham Valley Line, maintaining our railway museum and countryside centre, and providing an educational attraction for families, schools and visitors to Kent.

Connecting a Local Business With Kent Railway Heritage

The Elham Valley Line Trust was established in 1984 and is run entirely by volunteers. At the heart of our site is the railway museum, where visitors can step back in time to a recreated 1930s station and discover the story of the railway that once connected communities across the Elham Valley.

Visitors can also explore George's Barn, a 290-year-old countryside museum containing historic tools, crafts and household items, before following the Tangled Trail through the surrounding woodland.

The Vinyl Biz approached the Trust with an idea that connects its railway-inspired products with the preservation of genuine Kent railway heritage.

How Your Order Can Support the Trust

The Vinyl Biz produces a growing collection of UK-made train wall stickers and railway decals, featuring steam locomotives, railway silhouettes and personalised train designs.

To support the Elham Valley Line Trust:

  1. Visit The Vinyl Biz train wall sticker collection.
  2. Select your preferred design, size and colour.
  3. Enter the partnership code EVLT10 when ordering.
  4. The Vinyl Biz will donate 10% of the qualifying order value to the Trust.

There is no additional charge to the customer for the donation.

Helping Preserve a Piece of Kent's History

As a registered charity operated by volunteers, the Elham Valley Line Trust relies upon visitors, donations and community support. Partnerships with local businesses provide another way to help maintain the museum, preserve its collections and introduce the history of the Elham Valley Line to future generations.

We are grateful to The Vinyl Biz for choosing to support the Trust and for helping connect railway-themed creativity with the preservation of Kent's transport heritage.

You can explore the train wall sticker and railway decal collection and use code EVLT10 when ordering.

You can also plan your visit to the Elham Valley Line Trust and discover our railway museum, George's Barn and countryside attractions for yourself.

The 10% donation applies to qualifying orders placed using code EVLT10. The donation is calculated after discounts and excludes delivery charges, returns, refunds and cancelled orders.
